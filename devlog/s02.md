# s02 — a bigger café that builds itself

**Feature:**
1. The café grew from 9.2 m to 14 m wide. Furniture kept its size, so there's now room for 5 tables, two rugs, a lamp over every table, more shelves and plants. The outside scales up 1.4x to match.
2. **Build-up mode for reels:** every piece of the café is wrapped in `<Reveal id="…">`. In the reel, a piece pops in (springy overshoot) the moment the code line that builds it finishes typing.
3. `reel.html`: a 1080x1920 split screen with code typing on top and the café below, driven by a timeline (`src/reel/eps/s01.ts`).

**Lines worth showing** (`src/world/Reveal.tsx`): the pop.
```ts
// easeOutBack: overshoot a little, then settle = "pop"
const s = 1 + 2.4 * Math.pow(x - 1, 3) + 1.4 * Math.pow(x - 1, 2)
ref.current.scale.setScalar(s)
```
Concept: **easing curves**. Motion that overshoots feels alive. Linear motion feels like a robot.

**What broke:**
1. The walk camera sat at lamp height, so a pink lamp filled the screen. Raised it above the lamps.
2. Switching from barista view to walk left you stuck behind the counter. Now you step out onto the floor.
3. In the scaled-down reel preview, the 3D canvas measured its *scaled* size and only filled a corner. Fix: `resize={{ offsetSize: true }}` measures the real size.
